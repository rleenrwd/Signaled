# Denise Carter — Auditor Persona

## Profile

- **Role:** Auditor
- **Organization:** Large public-sector agency
- **Department:** Internal Audit & Compliance
- **Experience:** 7 years in audit and compliance review

## Background

Denise independently reviews investigations, decisions, and historical activity to verify that required procedures were followed and records are complete. She uses Signaled to examine case history, evidence, findings, and audit activity without modifying investigative records.

## Goals

- Verify investigations followed required procedures
- Review complete case histories efficiently
- Confirm important actions and decisions are traceable
- Identify missing or inconsistent documentation
- Access reliable records without altering them

## Pain Points

- Incomplete or fragmented audit trails
- Difficulty reconstructing who changed what and when
- Missing documentation behind important decisions
- Records spread across multiple systems
- Uncertainty about whether historical data has been altered

## Typical Tasks

- Review completed and active cases
- Examine findings and supporting evidence
- Review audit history and activity
- Search and filter historical records
- Verify assignments, status changes, and approvals
- Identify documentation or compliance issues

## Quote

> “I need to see a complete, trustworthy history of what happened without changing the record I’m reviewing.”
