# Jordan Lee — Contributor Persona

## Profile

- **Role:** Contributor
- **Organization:** Large public-sector agency
- **Department:** Operations
- **Experience:** 4 years in operational support

## Background

Jordan works in day-to-day operations and is often one of the first people to notice or receive information about an incident. Jordan uses Signaled to create event records, add supporting details, and provide investigators with accurate information when follow-up is needed.

## Goals

- Report incidents quickly and accurately
- Keep supporting information attached to the right event
- Know whether an issue has been received and reviewed
- Avoid repeating the same information across multiple systems

## Pain Points

- Unclear reporting processes
- Incident details scattered across email and spreadsheets
- Not knowing who owns an issue after submission
- Re-entering the same information multiple times

## Typical Tasks

- Create event records
- Add descriptions and supporting details
- Upload attachments or evidence
- Review events they are permitted to access
- Add follow-up information when requested

## Quote

> “I need a simple way to report what happened and make sure the right people have the information they need.”
