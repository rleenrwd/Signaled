# Maya Thompson — Investigator Persona

## Profile

- **Role:** Investigator
- **Organization:** Large public-sector agency
- **Department:** Operational Compliance & Investigations
- **Experience:** 3 years conducting formal investigations

## Background

Maya investigates operational incidents that require formal review. Events may originate from internal systems, employee reports, supervisors, or automated monitoring tools. Her job is to determine what happened, gather and evaluate supporting information, document findings, and bring assigned investigations to a defensible resolution.

She frequently works across departments and may need information from employees, supervisors, technical teams, or other operational systems.

## Goals

- Understand what happened and why
- Keep investigation records organized in one place
- Find related incidents quickly
- Document evidence, actions, and findings clearly
- Complete investigations with a traceable history

## Pain Points

- Information scattered across emails, spreadsheets, logs, and shared drives
- Difficult to reconstruct timelines
- Unclear ownership/status
- Hard to find similar past incidents
- Too much repetitive manual documentation

## Typical Tasks

- Review assigned events
- Open/manage cases
- Link related events
- Add notes and evidence
- Update case status
- Search historical incidents
- Record findings and resolution

## Quote

> “I need one reliable place to understand what happened, investigate it, and document how I reached my conclusion.”
