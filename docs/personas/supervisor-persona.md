# Elena Ramirez — Supervisor Persona

## Profile

- **Role:** Supervisor
- **Organization:** Large public-sector agency
- **Department:** Operational Compliance & Investigations
- **Experience:** 8 years in operations, 4 years supervising investigation teams

## Background

Elena oversees investigators and active cases. She uses Signaled to assign work, monitor case progress, review findings, and make sure investigations are completed consistently and on time.

## Goals

- Balance investigator workloads
- Keep cases moving toward resolution
- Review findings quickly and consistently
- Identify stalled or high-risk investigations
- Maintain visibility across team activity

## Pain Points

- Limited visibility into case progress
- Uneven workload distribution
- Difficulty spotting overdue investigations
- Inconsistent documentation between investigators
- Too much manual follow-up for status updates

## Typical Tasks

- Assign and reassign cases
- Review investigator workloads
- Monitor case status and timelines
- Review findings and evidence
- Approve closure or reopen cases
- Review dashboards and team trends

## Quote

> “I need a clear view of what my team is working on, what needs attention, and whether each investigation is ready to close.”
